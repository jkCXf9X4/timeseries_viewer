# Primary users

The viewer serves engineers and analysts who inspect logged or measured data.

- **Profile:** engineers and analysts who work with logged or measured data.
- **File pattern:** users who work with repeated local file sets and want a lightweight desktop workflow.
- **Method:** users who value direct visual inspection over notebook-style or server-backed analysis.
- **Platform:** users on Linux desktop environments.
