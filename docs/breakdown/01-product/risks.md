# Product risks

The main product risks sit at the edges of scale, time metadata, and expression usability.

- **Scale:** very large files may exceed the comfort zone of in-memory loading.
- **Time metadata:** time metadata may vary across sources and require user correction.
- **Usability:** expression usability may need refinement after real-world use.
